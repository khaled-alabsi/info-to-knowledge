# Public-domain portraits for the call-window and PDF figure tests

Each is a NASA photograph, a work of the US government and so in the public domain, fetched from Wikimedia Commons at
480 pixels wide. They are faces for the face detector in test scenes and photos of people in the test PDF of hard figures; nothing of
any meeting is in them. The last two were fetched at 960 pixels wide.
