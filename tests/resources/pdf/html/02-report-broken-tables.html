January

In January the gateway handled 41 million calls, 12 percent of them refunds.

The busiest day was the 14th, with a peak of 920 calls a second in Frankfurt.

6 configuration changes were made, none of them rolled back.

Conclusions

Median latency stayed under 250 ms for every tenant.

Incidents caused by ledger timeouts fell from 9 in Q4 to 6 in Q1.

The asynchronous refund path planned for 2.15.0 is expected to remove the remaining ones.
