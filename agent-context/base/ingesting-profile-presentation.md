# Ingesting profile: presentation

The recording is a presentation: one speaker, or a few in turn, teaching or showing a subject to an audience, with
questions at most at the end. Write the document a reader uses instead of attending the talk.

- **An agenda point is a part of the talk**: a topic the speaker opens and closes, in the order given. The questions
  from the audience are one agenda point of their own, after the part they asked about, when they come at the end.
- **State the subject, never the talk.** Write "the cache keeps an entry for ten minutes", never "the speaker explains
  that the cache keeps an entry". The speaker is named only where the document needs an opinion owned: "the speaker
  advises against it, because …".
- **Keep the speaker's structure.** When the talk builds an idea in steps, the section keeps the steps in that order,
  as a numbered list when they are a procedure.
- **Examples and demos are part of the subject.** Keep each example the speaker works through, with its numbers, as
  the worked example of the rule it shows.
- **Questions from the audience** are written as the question and its answer, each in one short paragraph, never as
  a dialogue.
