## Step: the agenda items

Find the agenda items the meeting goes through, in the whole speech.

- **An agenda item is one subject the meeting discusses**: the announced agenda's points when there is one, otherwise
  each subject the talk turns to. Title it with the subject.
- **Each item once**, in the order the meeting first takes it; a return to it later is cited under the same item.
- **Every paragraph belongs to at least one item.** The opening, small talk and the close go to the item nearest them
  in time.
- `### What it is about`: the subject and what the meeting wants from it, in one or two sentences.
