## Step: the lesson units

Find the units the lesson works through, in the whole speech.

- **A unit is one block of the lesson with one aim**: a set of words, a grammar point, a dialogue practised, an
  exercise. Title it with what it practises.
- **Each unit once**, in the order the lesson takes it; a return to it later is cited under the same unit.
- **Every paragraph belongs to at least one unit.** Greetings and organisation go to the unit nearest them in time.
- `### What it practises`: the aim of the unit, in one or two sentences.
