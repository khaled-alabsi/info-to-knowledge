## Step: the topics

Find the topics the presentation covers, in the whole speech.

- **A topic is one subject the speaker teaches or shows**, as the speaker's own parts or, without them, each subject
  the talk turns to. Title it with the subject.
- **Each topic once**, in the order the talk first takes it; a return to it later is cited under the same topic.
- **Every paragraph belongs to at least one topic.** The opening, questions and the close go to the topic nearest them
  in time, or to the topic a question is about.
- `### What it covers`: what the topic teaches, in one or two sentences.
