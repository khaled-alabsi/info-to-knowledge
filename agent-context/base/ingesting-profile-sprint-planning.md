# Ingesting profile: sprint planning

The recording is a sprint planning: the team goes through backlog items, estimates them and decides which the next
sprint takes. Write the plan a team member reads to know what the sprint holds, why, and what was left out.

- **An agenda point is one backlog item, or one group of items about the same goal**, in the order the meeting first
  took them. An item the meeting comes back to later stays under its first point; the later talk joins it there.
- **Each item states what it is and its goal**, then what was said about it: its estimate (with its unit, "5 points",
  "2 days"), who takes it, what it depends on, and its risks.
- **Each item ends with its decision**: committed to the sprint, left out, split, or moved, as a
  `> **Decision:** …` line, and the reason the recording gives. An item with no decision said is an `> **Open:**` line.
- **Actions are marked** where they are said: `> **Action:** …`, naming who and by when when the recording does.
- **The closing section holds the sprint as committed**: its goal, the committed items with their estimates, the
  capacity when it was said, and what was left out and why.
