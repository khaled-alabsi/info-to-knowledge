# Ingesting profile: sprint change

The recording is about changing a sprint's plan: work added, removed, moved or re-estimated, and why. Write the
change record a team member reads to know what the sprint now holds and why it changed.

- **An agenda point is one change, or one group of changes about the same goal**, in the order the meeting took them.
- **Each change states what changed, from what to what**: the item, the change (added, removed, moved to a later
  sprint, re-estimated, split), the old and the new value where there is one ("estimate 3 → 8 points").
- **Each change keeps its reason** and who asked for it, as the recording states them.
- **Each change names what it affects**: other items, the sprint goal, a date, another team. Unknown impact is an
  `> **Open:**` line.
- **Decisions and actions are marked** where they are said: `> **Decision:** …`, `> **Action:** …`.
- **Close each section with the state after the change** when the recording gives it: what the sprint now holds for
  that goal.
