# Ingesting profile: language lesson

The recording is a language lesson: a teacher and one or more learners, or a learner practising alone, working on a
language the learner is learning. Write the study notes a learner reads to review the lesson.

- **An agenda point is a unit of the lesson**: a topic, a grammar point, a text read together, an exercise.
- **The document's language is the language the lesson is held in**, the one the teacher explains in, as the transcript
  shows it, never English unless the lesson is held in English: every heading, explanation, table and summary. A lesson
  explained in German about Spanish words is written in German, its Spanish words kept as they are.
- **Keep the taught language as it is taught.** Every word, phrase and example sentence of the language being learned
  is written in that language, exactly, never translated in its place.
- **Vocabulary is a table**: the word or phrase, its meaning, and a note (gender, plural, a usage hint) where the
  lesson gives one. One table per unit, with every word the lesson taught in it.
- **Grammar is a rule plus examples**: state the rule in one or two sentences, then the lesson's own example sentences
  as a list, each with its meaning.
- **Corrections are worth keeping.** When the teacher corrects a learner, write the wrong form and the right form side
  by side, and the reason when it was given: "~~ich habe gegangen~~ → ich bin gegangen: a verb of movement takes sein".
- **Exercises** keep their task and their solution, so the learner can do them again.
