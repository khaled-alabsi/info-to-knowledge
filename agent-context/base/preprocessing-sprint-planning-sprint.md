## Step: the sprint as committed

Write the whole sprint as the meeting leaves it, as one item titled `The sprint`. The items are listed above, as the
earlier steps wrote them: use them to know what was committed, and cite the paragraphs that say it.

- `### Sprint goal`: the goal the team sets for the sprint, in its own words.
- `### Committed items`: each item the sprint takes, with its estimate, as a list.
- `### Capacity`: the team's capacity for the sprint and how the committed estimates compare with it, as said.
- `### Left out`: each item left out or moved, and why.
