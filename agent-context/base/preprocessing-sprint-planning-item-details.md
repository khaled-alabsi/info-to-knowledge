## Step: one item in detail

Write out the one item this request is about, from its paragraphs and from what the first step wrote about it.

- `### Goal`: what the item is for, and what is done when it is done.
- `### Estimate`: every estimate said, with its unit ("5 points", "2 days"), and who gave it; a changed estimate with
  both values and why it changed.
- `### Owner`: who takes the item, or who was asked to.
- `### Dependencies and risks`: what the item waits on, what waits on it, and every risk said.
- `### Decision`: committed to the sprint, left out, split or moved, and the reason given.
- `### Open points`: every question about the item the meeting left open, each as one sentence.
