## Step: one change in detail

Write out the one change this request is about, from its paragraphs and from what the first step wrote about it.

- `### Before and after`: the old and the new value of everything the change moves (scope, estimate, date, sprint).
- `### Reason`: why the change is made, as said.
- `### Asked by`: who asked for the change.
- `### Impact`: what the change affects: other items, the sprint goal, a date, another team.
- `### Decision`: whether the change was accepted, and any condition set on it.
