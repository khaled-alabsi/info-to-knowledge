## Step: one agenda item in detail

Write out the one agenda item this request is about, from its paragraphs and from what the first step wrote about it.

- `### Decisions`: every decision taken on the item, each as one sentence with its reason when one is given.
- `### Actions`: every action agreed, each as one sentence naming who does it and by when, as said.
- `### Open points`: every question the meeting left open on the item, each as one sentence.
