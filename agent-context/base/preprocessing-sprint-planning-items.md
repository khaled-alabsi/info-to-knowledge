## Step: the items discussed

Find the backlog items the sprint planning talks about, in the whole speech.

- **An item is one piece of work the team plans**: a story, a task, a bug, or a group of tasks the team treats as one.
  Title it as the team names it.
- **Each item once**, in the order the meeting first takes it. When the meeting comes back to an item later, cite the
  later paragraphs under the same item.
- **Every paragraph belongs to at least one item.** Small talk, the meeting's opening and its close go to the item
  nearest them in time.
- `### What it is`: what the work is and why the team wants it, in one or two sentences.
