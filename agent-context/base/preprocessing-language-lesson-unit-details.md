## Step: one unit in detail

Write out the one unit this request is about, from its paragraphs and from what the first step wrote about it. Keep
every word and sentence of the language being learned exactly as it is spoken in the paragraphs, with its meaning
when the lesson gives one.

- `### Vocabulary`: each word or phrase taught, as a list: the word, its meaning, and an example sentence when said.
- `### Grammar`: each rule taught, with the examples given.
- `### Corrections`: each mistake corrected, as the wrong form, the right form and why, when said.
- `### Exercises`: each exercise done, and the learner's answers that were right.
