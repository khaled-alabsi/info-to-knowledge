# The lines a PDF export prints around its content and no reader needs: one regular expression a line, matched against
# a whole line of a page's text, case ignored, bold and underline marks and digits as they stand. Read by
# services/pdf_source_service.py; every line a pattern removes is written to 01_cleanup.json with the pattern.
# A wiki export's chrome
^(#+\s*)?page tree\b.*
^(\S{1,3}\s+)?created by .*
^last updated by .*
^.*\b\d+ views?$
^jump to .*
^.*\bback to top$
^like\b.*people like this$
^\S{0,8}\s*[-·|]?\s*\d+ (people|person) likes? this$
^labels:.*
^attachments\s*(\(\d+\))?$
^(edit|like|share|watch|save for later)(\s*[·|]\s*(edit|like|share|watch|save for later))*$
^printed from .*
^page \d+ of \d+$
